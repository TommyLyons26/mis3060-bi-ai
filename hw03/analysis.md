# Based on your data, do the executive changes you found appear to precede or follow earnings announcements? Is there any pattern across the five companies, or does it vary?

The executive changes I found tend to come before earnings announcements, and not after: 21 before, 6 after and 2 in the same week. At JP Morgan and Walmart, every change came before earnings or in the same week. At Apple and Nvidia, most of their changes came before earnings, with two after earnings for both of these companies. Microsoft is the exception here, with most of their changes coming after earnings.
